# HANDOVER.md Format

`HANDOVER.md` is the journey's outbound artifact — written once by the advisor at the arrival pass ([ARRIVAL.md](ARRIVAL.md)), read by whoever picks the learner up next: a mentor-shaped skill, a human, a future course's advisor. It is **receiver-agnostic**: every duty a receiver must honour travels inside the document, so it presumes no particular reader.

Where it lands, and the exchange that confirms it, are [ARRIVAL.md](ARRIVAL.md)'s. When the course-root fallback applies, add one line under the title: this document travels with the build — moved together with it.

## Template

```md
# Handover: {course title}

## To the receiver

{Two duties, stated to whoever reads this. **Read back**: restate this document to the learner and have it corrected before acting on it. **Observe first**: direct observation of the learner's real work comes before this document counts as evidence. You are meeting this learner with zero prior contact — exactly where a secondhand expectation distorts hardest.}

## Placement

{One coarse sentence — triage, never diagnosis.}

## The journey

{The destination sentence, and under it the verified capability list — the mapping produced by running the arrival checklist, carried verbatim into both this section and the arrival log entry.}

## Capability → gap map

{What the destination still implies that the journey didn't close. Subject and evidence, never the cure, the sequence, or the duration — those are the receiver's to design with the learner.}

- {Gap} — evidence: {the record line or audit finding, described}

## Contingencies

{"If X recurs, the cure that worked was Y" — mined from the log and the records' Signals. The slot a summary always omits, and the one a receiver can least reconstruct.}

## Terminology

{The course's coined words the learner will use — each with its one-line meaning.}

## Suggested skills

{What still serves the learner — `/office-hours` still answers against the retired course. Add what the journey suggests.}

## Learner annotations

{The learner's own words from the arrival exchange, verbatim — their section, never merged into the ones above.}
```

## Rules

- **~2K token cap.** The document must survive being distilled into an intake slot roughly half its size; a handover that needs summarizing before it fits has failed its one job.
- **Distills, never points.** No paths into the course folder, no "see `.records/`": the course folder is a retired archive, and a receiver's own rules may bar it from following the pointer — whatever matters travels in the document itself. This deliberately inverts the reference-over-duplication rule that holds everywhere else in the suite.
- **Evidence and observation, never a grade.** The register is the record register: what happened, what was seen, readable by the learner without feeling judged. Counterfeit: evaluative adjectives — "struggled," "poorly," "still weak" — smuggling a verdict into an evidence line. The roadmap page's stronger rule — the learner never meets the sentence that diagnosed them — deliberately does **not** extend here: the gap map's whole value is that the learner reads it, corrects it, and carries it knowingly.
- **Annotations are verbatim, their own section, never merged** — disagreement is data the receiver needs, not noise to reconcile away. The exchange that collects them is [ARRIVAL.md](ARRIVAL.md)'s.
