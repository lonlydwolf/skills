# roadmap.html

The content contract for the learner's visual roadmap, at the course root. When
it is regenerated, what it renders from, and the guardrails that bind it are in
[SKILL.md](SKILL.md#roadmaphtml); this file says what the page must hold.

The page opens with a comment saying it is a render of `roadmap.md`, redrawn by
the advisor at every stamp, never edited by hand and never a second source of
truth. Every render carries it: under the **seed** rule a hand edit propagates
into every later render, so the page's own defence against being edited is
load-bearing rather than decorative.

The page title is `Roadmap — {course name}`. The lede says what the page is: a
map of the journey, not a report card — nothing here measures the learner. It
also says plainly what finishing means: delivery, not certification — the build
after arrival is where capability shows itself.

## Never an inventory

`index.html` lists the units and carries every link. This page never repeats
that list: the hub says where everything is, and this page says **why**.

**Translated, never quoted.** A `Why:` line states a deficit — *"Learner-now
lacks X"* — and outcomes are phrased for the advisor. The page says what the
unit **made possible**. A learner reading their own plan never meets the
sentence that diagnosed them.

**The decisions rendered are the course's, never the learner's.** `roadmap.md`'s
Decisions-so-far holds two natures: the planning-ticket gists — the territory
calls behind what is being built — belong here; a checkpoint plan delta
(*"unit 04 inserted — the audit found a gap in Y"*) never does. The second is
judgment wearing a plan's clothes.

## What it holds

Where the course is going · where it is now and why this unit · what the
earlier units made possible · what's next · the fog · the shape it holds itself
to. Each follows `roadmap.md`; a section with nothing behind it is left out
rather than left empty.

## Tags

Two words, closed: `now` for the current unit, `next` for a frontier line. They
say **where something sits, never how well it went** — never a status, a grade,
a completion tick, a percentage, or a count. Both are redrawn with the whole
page at the stamp, never hand-moved.

## Arrival

The final stamp renders the page journey-complete: the destination in the past
tense with the capabilities that prove it, the whole accumulated why, and the
decisions that got there. The `now` tag goes too — no unit is current in an
arrived course. Still journey, never judgment — see [ARRIVAL.md](ARRIVAL.md).
