# roadmap.html

The template for the learner's visual roadmap. Regenerated wholesale at every
stamp phase — **a render, never a second source**. Never hand-edited, so nothing
here needs to survive being patched.

The three guardrails in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md#roadmaphtml) bind this page: render
the fog honestly, journey never judgment, styled with the course theme. This
file only says what the markup is.

Shell per the page kit
([../teach-unit/PAGE-KIT.md](../teach-unit/PAGE-KIT.md)) — the same vocabulary
every learner-facing page speaks — in its **course-root** variant: no prefix on
any path, and the breadcrumb is one hop to the hub. Only this page's own parts
are below.

Keep the file comment: it is the page's own defence against being hand-edited.

```html
<!--
  roadmap.html — a render of roadmap.md, regenerated by the advisor at every
  stamp. Never edited by hand, never a second source of truth.
-->

  <h1>📍 Roadmap</h1>
  <p class="lede">{{COURSE_NAME}} — {{THE_BUILD}}. This page is redrawn as the
  course is taught. It is a map of the journey, not a report card: nothing here
  measures you.</p>

  <!-- no `numbered` on <main>: these sections are the shape of a journey, not
       steps to work through -->
  <main id="main">

    <h2>Where we're going</h2>

    <div class="card">
      <p><strong>{{DESTINATION — the capability, stated as something the learner
      will be able to do}}</strong></p>
      <p><strong>The proof:</strong> {{THE_BUILD — what will exist at the end}}</p>
    </div>

    <h2>Where we are</h2>

    <div class="card">
      <h3><span class="tag">now</span> Unit {{NN}} — {{UNIT_NAME}}</h3>
      <p>{{WHAT_IT_MAKES_TRUE — the unit's outcome in the learner's own terms}}</p>
      <p class="muted">Why this now: {{THE_REASON}}</p>
    </div>

    <h2>What's next</h2>

    <!-- One card per frontier line. One or two — never a numbered promise-list
         of future units: the rolling design is taught by this render, not
         betrayed by it. -->
    <div class="card">
      <h3><span class="tag">next</span> {{FRONTIER_LINE}}</h3>
      <p>{{WHY_IT_FOLLOWS}}</p>
    </div>

    <p class="muted">Only the next step or two is ever drawn sharply. What comes
    after depends on what the next unit actually turns up — planning further
    would be guessing, and the guess would be wrong.</p>

    <h2>Somewhere out in the fog</h2>

    <p class="muted">Themes, not a schedule. Unordered on purpose — some will
    become units, some will turn out to be unnecessary, some don't exist yet.</p>

    <ul>
      <li>{{FOG_THEME — coarse and deliberately vague}}</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>The build</h2>

    <p>{{THE_BUILD — one paragraph: what it is, and what it does}}</p>

    <details>
      <summary>Version one — what's in, and what it refuses</summary>
      <p><strong>In:</strong> {{SCOPE}}</p>
      <p><strong>Refused, out loud, with a reason:</strong> {{OUT_OF_SCOPE}} —
      {{WHY_REFUSING_IS_A_FEATURE}}</p>
    </details>

    <h3>Milestones</h3>

    <ol>
      <li>{{MILESTONE}} <span class="muted">{{OPTIONAL — where the hard part
        lives}}</span></li>
    </ol>

    <h2>Shape</h2>

    <ul>
      <li><strong>{{CONSTRAINT}}.</strong> {{WHY_IT_HOLDS}}</li>
    </ul>

  </main>
```

The page title is `Roadmap — {{COURSE_NAME}}`.

## Done units

Units already taught appear under **Where we are**, above the current one, as
plain cards with no tag — being earlier is not a status. Never a checkmark, a
completion count, a percentage, or an attainment mark of any kind: the page
renders the *plan*, and the auditor's verdicts are not the learner's to read
here.

## Arrival

At the final stamp this page becomes a journey-complete render: every unit
listed, **What's next** and **Somewhere out in the fog** gone entirely rather
than left empty, and the lede rewritten in the past tense. **The `now` tag goes
with them** — no unit is current in an arrived course, and a finished course
still tagging one turns position into a claim. Still journey, never judgment —
see [ARRIVAL.md](ARRIVAL.md).
