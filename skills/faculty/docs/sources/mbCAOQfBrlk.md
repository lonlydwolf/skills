## Contribution

Preview–Predict–Purpose activates context before reading: inspect the text, form a provisional prediction and define a reading goal. The source also warns that misleading prior assumptions can distort comprehension.

## Faculty application

- Label whether framing comes from the text, publisher, reviewer, teacher or AI.
- Keep predictions provisional, including an alternative and a disconfirming cue.
- Supply relevant bridging knowledge without assuming a shared cultural background.
- Revisit the frame when the text conflicts with it.
- Check comprehension through evidence-grounded explanation and application.

## Evidence boundaries

Context experiments support a principle, not the exact routine as a universal package or guaranteed instant benefit. Covers and external reviews can bias interpretation or spoil intended discovery; learners can choose preview depth. Accurate context matters more than a feeling of coherence. The captions misname the authors of the false-schema paper; the bibliography identifies Oded and Stavans.
