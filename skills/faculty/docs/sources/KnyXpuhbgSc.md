# 11 Tiny Habits To Learn and Think Like The Top 1%

Creator: Justin Sung · Duration: 19:44 · Published: 2026-08-14

[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KnyXpuhbgSc)

## Contribution

The source recommends generating provisional explanations before consuming answers, elaborating relationships, diagnosing gaps early and seeking disconfirming questions. It frames learning as building and revising a model rather than collecting information.

## Faculty application

- Invite a reachable initial attempt and preserve it as provisional.
- Ask why a mechanism works and where an analogy stops working.
- Use early diagnostics and source-hidden retrieval to locate the boundary.
- Select a next question that matters to the learner's goal.
- Treat boredom as a cue to investigate purpose, challenge and conditions.

## Evidence boundaries

The habits are not established by representative measurements of elite learners. Coaching experience does not prove causality. Generation needs feedback and scaffolding, especially for novices. Boredom may reflect fatigue, difficulty, low challenge or other circumstances; it is not biological proof that no learning is occurring. More difficulty is not automatically more useful.
