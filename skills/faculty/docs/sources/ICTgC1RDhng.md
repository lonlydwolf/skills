# You Were The Smart Kid... So Why Are You Lost?

Creator: Mark Manson · Duration: 15:24 · Published: 2025-12-21

[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTgC1RDhng)

## Contribution

The video describes how a fixed “smart person” identity can discourage uncertain attempts, help-seeking and revision. It suggests attending to process and controllable actions rather than treating an early label as a permanent explanation of performance.

## Faculty application

- Make low-stakes attempts, uncertainty and asking for help acceptable.
- Give feedback about a particular strategy and observed result.
- Preserve standards while making correction and retry recoverable.
- Invite learners to revise assumptions about what they need, without assigning a personality diagnosis.

## Evidence boundaries

Gifted learners are heterogeneous. The video does not establish that a “smart kid” background causes adult difficulty, and biographies or IQ anecdotes do not establish causality. Process feedback should not become empty praise for effort regardless of outcome. Public failure is a learner choice, not a compulsory exercise. Sponsored product material is not educational evidence.
