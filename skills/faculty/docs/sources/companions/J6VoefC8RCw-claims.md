# Japanese-mastery claim boundaries

[Video synthesis](../J6VoefC8RCw.md)

[Di Stefano and colleagues' reflection paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2414478) supports studying reflection under particular conditions, including workplace training. A reported improvement in that setting is not a universal percentage for learners or subjects.

[Kumon's institutional history](https://www.kumon.com/about-kumon/history) describes its own development; it does not establish comparative effectiveness for every learner. The creator also supplies a [source/correction page](https://betteru.live/sources/japanese-mastery/).

Kata, Shu-Ha-Ri, hansei and other terms have different origins and contexts. Faculty adopts explicit practice, scaffold fading, recoverable errors and evidence-based reflection. It does not claim one national learning system, compulsory cultural ritual or centuries-old experimental validation.

Compounding arithmetic illustrates accumulation under assumed conditions; it does not model skill improvement as a constant daily rate. Historical quotations and broad Honda or school-cleaning anecdotes were not independently established. Keep them out of effectiveness claims.
