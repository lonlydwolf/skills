# Study-method attribution corrections

[Video synthesis](../7aPtWCvqJi8.md)

[Francesco Cirillo's official account](https://www.pomodorotechnique.com/francesco-cirillo/) places Pomodoro in its Italian development context. [Retrieval research](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1152408) supports active recall without a national-secret attribution.

[Toyota's explanation of nemawashi](https://mag.toyota.co.uk/nemawashi-toyota-production-system/) concerns organizational preparation and consensus. Applying it to prereading is an adaptation, not its original educational meaning.

Kaizen has Japanese origins, but national attribution does not establish an intervention's effect. Shuchuryoku denotes concentration rather than a unique validated method. A rule of three can organize a session but is not a universal memory law. The standardized Feynman Technique attribution remains uncertain.

Keep the practical behaviors (prepare, focus, retrieve, explain, check, adjust) while removing misleading provenance. Affiliate supplement promotion is separate from the learning synthesis and supplies no basis for health advice.
