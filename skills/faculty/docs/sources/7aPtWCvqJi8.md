## Contribution

The AI-narrated video collects preparation, bounded focus, retrieval, explanation, planning and incremental improvement. These are usable study behaviors when adapted to a learner, but the advertised national framing mixes unrelated methods.

## Faculty application

- Prepare a small task and specify what completion looks like.
- Let focus intervals adapt to the learner and task.
- Retrieve, explain and verify instead of counting rereading as mastery.
- Use one manageable improvement grounded in the previous attempt.

## Evidence boundaries

The title advertises seven methods while the narration presents eight. Pomodoro is associated with Francesco Cirillo, active recall with cognitive research, and nemawashi with organizational groundwork; grouping them as Japanese secrets is misleading. Shuchuryoku is ordinary concentration vocabulary, and a rule of three is not a universal learning law. Affiliate supplement promotion provides no basis for health guidance.
