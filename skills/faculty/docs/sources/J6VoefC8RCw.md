## Contribution

The creator combines kata, Shu-Ha-Ri, poka-yoke, hansei, kaizen and shokunin into a practice narrative. The reusable pattern is model a move, practice it, inspect errors, reflect on a specific result and improve one component before increasing independence.

## Faculty application

- Use worked models and scaffold fading according to observed readiness.
- Design recoverable practice mistakes with feedback and a corrected retry.
- Ask for concrete reflection tied to evidence rather than a ceremonial ritual.
- Use narrow improvement targets and an appropriate performance standard.

## Evidence boundaries

These terms come from distinct traditions; the video does not establish a universal Japanese mastery system. National generalizations, historical quotations and impressive numerical claims need separate evidence. One workplace reflection study and Kumon's institutional history do not establish effectiveness across all learners or tasks. Faculty uses operational learning patterns, not cultural authority as proof.
