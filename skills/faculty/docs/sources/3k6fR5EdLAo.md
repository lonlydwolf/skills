## Contribution

The ALTER framework separates planning, resource selection, understanding, production and exploratory conversation. It provides the starting role vocabulary for Faculty: Advisor, Librarian, Tutor, Editor and Roommate. The important contribution is making different educational jobs explicit rather than expecting one undifferentiated conversation to handle them all.

## Faculty application

- Keep course planning, teaching, evaluation, artifact review and exploration distinct.
- Turn a learning ambition into a concrete capability and learner-produced evidence.
- Treat resource curation as support for learning rather than completion by itself.
- Faculty adds Teacher as a separate instructional owner; its workflow is an adopted design, not a transcription of ALTER.

## Evidence boundaries

This is a conceptual tutorial, not a controlled comparison of AI education with teachers or universities. The speaker's MOOC, Stanford, Bloom and Pixar figures were not independently established during ingestion. Do not publish those anecdotes as Faculty effectiveness evidence. A role label or a fluent explanation does not demonstrate mastery.
