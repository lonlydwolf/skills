## Contribution

The video combines previewing, focus and rest, retrieval, explanation, useful difficulty, consistent setup and low-stakes attempts. Much overlaps earlier sources; its contribution is a practical session sequence rather than a new learning theory.

## Faculty application

- Begin with the target performance and a bounded session output.
- Adapt session length and recovery to actual learner conditions.
- Use retrieval and corrected explanation after studying a model.
- Fade scaffolds when evidence supports independence.
- Revisit the target after a delay and under changed conditions.

## Evidence boundaries

Fixed intervals and dramatic speed claims are not universal. Struggle is not useful by itself, and novices may benefit from worked examples. “Try before ready” belongs in recoverable practice, not unsafe procedures. Do not turn broad advice about sleep, food or recovery into medical prescriptions. Repeated concepts should reinforce a coherent workflow rather than add a long mandatory ritual.
