## Contribution

The source organizes reading around reusable operations, challenges to beliefs and warnings about mistakes. Its recurring themes include separating luck from skill, revising beliefs, making decisions under uncertainty and recognizing rationalization.

## Faculty application

- Select readings for the learner's goal, background, access and cost.
- Ask for an applied operation or evidence-grounded argument after reading.
- Identify what could disconfirm the learner's interpretation.
- Distinguish decision quality from the outcome of one uncertain event.
- Keep the seven books as optional resources, not prerequisites for every course.

## Evidence boundaries

“Top 1%,” rewiring, prediction totals and numerical financial anecdotes are promotional or unverified here. Simplified business stories do not establish general laws. Research discussed in popular psychology can have replication and interpretation limits. Finishing pages is not demonstrated capability. The companion preserves the bibliography and a usable artifact cycle.
