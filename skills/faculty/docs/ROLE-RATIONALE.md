# Why the roles are separate

[Faculty design](DESIGN.md) describes the architecture. This short rationale explains why each boundary matters.

## Advisor

Planning and curriculum changes need a single owner. Advisor turns an ambition into prerequisites, valid unit contracts and an evidence-supported current learner profile. Rolling-wave planning avoids spending effort on distant units before the learner's progress is known. Immutable activated contracts prevent criteria from moving after the learner has started.

## Teacher

Instruction needs room to adapt while keeping the target stable. Teacher chooses explanations, scaffolds and practice inside the contract, preserves observations and produces usable learner materials. Separating Teacher from Tutor prevents helpful instruction from being mistaken for independent evidence. Explicit assignment contracts let learners and reviewers work against the same requirements.

## Tutor

Familiarity and fluency can resemble understanding. Tutor asks the learner to demonstrate conceptual capability and records all critical criteria, including missing evidence. LESSON checks give timely repair, MILESTONE checks integrate intermediate concepts and UNIT checks support durable consolidation. New immutable attempts preserve progress without rewriting failures.

## Editor

A correct explanation does not ensure a useful artifact. Editor inspects BUILD outputs in their intended context, records consequences and derives a verdict from material findings. Keeping revisions with the learner preserves the learning task. Requiring conceptual evidence for a foundational referral prevents every implementation mistake from becoming a reteaching detour.

## Librarian

Source quality and instructional suitability are distinct jobs. A dedicated worker can research, qualify claims and preserve citations while the invoker remains responsible for whether that research fits the unit. Waiting for saved accepted results prevents a proposal from relying on research that merely started. Append-only entries retain the source trail when corrections are needed.

## Roommate

Curiosity deserves space without turning every tangent into curriculum or evaluation. Roommate offers reciprocal exploration and unusual connections from learner-supplied context. Leaving course files untouched protects that freedom. Structural analogy checks prevent a vivid comparison from silently becoming a false inference.

## Initialization and shared rules

Initialization creates a consistent workspace only after checking that every required role is available. The shared ledger carries enough routing state for fresh sessions, while instructional evidence stays in owned records. Saved read-back and fresh UTC timestamps address observed publication failures; a single bundled rules file keeps the fix consistent across writers.

The [source library](sources/README.md) records intellectual inputs and their limits. Useful Japanese practice terms, Socratic questioning, TILT, GRASPS and Oxford-inspired defense remain local teaching concepts, with domain-specific limits rather than universal effectiveness claims.
