# deck.html

The content contract for a vocabulary unit's deck —
`units/NN-<slug>/lessons/deck.html`, the term/definition source the recall page
regenerates from, and the central artifact of the vocabulary profile
([profiles/vocabulary.md](profiles/vocabulary.md)).

**Both directions, always.** Two stacks over the same terms with the cues
reversed, one `<h2>` each — term→meaning, then meaning→term. A deck that only
runs term→meaning teaches recognition, which is the floor. The two shuffle
independently, so a second pass is not the first pass in order.

A meaning is written in plain words — the way you would say it out loud, not the
way the source text puts it.

The page title is `Unit {{NN}} · Deck — {{UNIT_NAME}}`. Its headings are not
numbered — the two stacks are directions, not steps.

## Refreshing

The deck grows within its unit as terms are taught, and is rewritten rather than
appended to when a term's wording changes — a deck with two phrasings of one
term teaches the phrasing, not the term.

It is a practice instrument, and the zero-stakes firewall applies to it in full.
